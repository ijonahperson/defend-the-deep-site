Defend the Deep 3D: Support

Stuck on a site, found a bug or have an idea? Send a message here with your iPhone model and iOS version, and say which site you were on. I read every message.

Tips

  • Armoured enemies shrug off the vulcan, so bring missiles.
  • A teal shield bubble pops to the laser.
  • The arc chains through swarms.
  • Your shield recharges after a few seconds out of fire; your hull does not, so retreating early keeps your salvage.

Saving your progress

Progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
