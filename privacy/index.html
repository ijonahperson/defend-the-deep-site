Privacy Policy for Defend the Deep 3D

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Defend the Deep 3D does not collect, store or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your progress (sites cleared, scrap, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your device. It is removed when you delete the app. If your device backs up apps to iCloud, Apple handles that backup under its own privacy policy.

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.
